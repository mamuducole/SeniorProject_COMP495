Privacy Policy

This page explains how THE ROCKS, Inc. and ClubExpress collect, use, protect, and manage personal information.

Revised June 11, 2021
THE ROCKS, Inc. uses ClubExpress to support membership, events, communications, donations, and website services. ClubExpress serves as the platform provider and data processor for information collected through these services.

Information We Collect

Personal information may be collected when users interact with the website, register for events, donate, volunteer, apply for programs, or participate in organizational activities.

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Membership and account information
  • Event registrations and payment history
  • Donation and volunteer records
  • Website activity, logins, and communication preferences

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the organization, provide services, maintain accurate records, communicate with members and supporters, and support nonprofit activities.

  • Managing memberships and programs
  • Processing donations and event registrations
  • Sending organizational updates and announcements
  • Supporting scholarships, mentorship, and leadership initiatives
  • Improving website performance, safety, and reliability

Data Protection

ClubExpress uses technical and operational safeguards to help protect personal information. Sensitive information such as passwords and payment data is protected through secure systems and encryption.

While no online system is completely risk-free, THE ROCKS, Inc. and ClubExpress work to maintain a secure and trustworthy online environment.

Sharing of Information

Personal information may be shared only when necessary to operate the organization, provide services, process payments, manage events, support vendors, comply with legal requirements, or protect platform security.

ClubExpress does not sell personal information for marketing or revenue purposes.

Your Rights and Choices

Members may log in to view and update personal information, change privacy settings, and manage communication preferences. Non-members may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information through the organization’s contact process.

Users may also request that their information be deleted, subject to the organization’s need to maintain accurate legal, financial, and operational records.

Cookies and Website Data

The website may use cookies, log files, IP addresses, browser information, and related technologies to support login sessions, improve performance, protect security, and understand website usage.

Mobile App Permissions

If ClubExpress mobile features are enabled, the app may request permissions such as calendar, notifications, storage, camera, phone, or location. These permissions are used only for app-related features and user-selected actions.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information is collected, how it is used, whether it is shared, and the right to request deletion.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not intended for unsupervised access by children under 13. Personal information from children under 13 is not knowingly collected.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or data practices may be submitted through the Contact Us page or directed to ClubExpress.
