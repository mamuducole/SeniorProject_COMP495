Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about mentorship, scholarships, donations, and how to get involved with THE ROCKS, Inc.

General Organization FAQs

What is THE ROCKS, Inc?

THE ROCKS, Inc. is a nonprofit organization dedicated to mentorship, leadership development, scholarship opportunities, and professional growth.

Who does THE ROCKS, Inc. serve?

We support students, young professionals, emerging leaders, and individuals seeking mentorship, networking, and career development opportunities.

Is THE ROCKS, Inc. a nonprofit organization?

Yes. THE ROCKS, Inc. is a registered nonprofit organization committed to empowering future leaders through mentorship and service.

Mentorship FAQs

How do I apply for a mentor?

You can apply through our mentorship application form on the website. Once submitted, our team reviews applications and matches mentees based on goals, interests, and availability.

Who can become a mentee?

Students, young professionals, and individuals seeking guidance in leadership, career development, academics, or personal growth are encouraged to apply.

What kinds of topics can mentors help with?
  • Leadership development
  • Career planning
  • College preparation
  • Networking
  • Professional confidence
  • Goal setting
  • Work-life balance
Can mentorship meetings be virtual?

Yes. Many mentor-mentee meetings are offered virtually to increase accessibility.

Volunteer & Mentor FAQs

How can I become a mentor?

Potential mentors can complete the mentor interest form on our website. Applicants may go through a review or onboarding process.

What qualifications do mentors need?

We look for individuals with leadership experience, professionalism, strong communication skills, and a passion for helping others succeed.

Scholarships & Programs FAQs

Does THE ROCKS, Inc. offer scholarships?

Yes. Scholarship opportunities may be available to eligible students based on program criteria and funding availability.

What other programs do you offer?

We offer leadership workshops, networking events, community service initiatives, professional development sessions, and educational programming.

Donations & Support FAQs

How can I support THE ROCKS, Inc.?

You can support our mission through donations, volunteering, mentorship, sponsorships, or community partnerships.

Where do donations go?

Donations help fund mentorship programs, scholarships, leadership initiatives, events, and community outreach efforts.

Still have questions?

Contact us and a member of our team will follow up with you.
