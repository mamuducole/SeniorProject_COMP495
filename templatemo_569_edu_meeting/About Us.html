About Us

Since the mid-70s, The ROCKS, an international 501(c)3 nonprofit organization, through its National Board, chapters and Interest Groups has been a source of strength within the military community, providing mentorship and professional leadership development to military officers.

Upon reaching Washington, several informal meetings followed. However, the emphasis was modified to deal with survival in the National Capitol Region and to address other issues of interest to the members.
